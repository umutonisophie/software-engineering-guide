Product Management

Product management decides what gets built and, more importantly, can explain why. It is the discipline of choosing between options when every one of them has a constituency.

Prioritising is the actual job

Requests arrive from sales, support, engineering and users, each with a legitimate claim. Choosing means weighing reach, effort, risk and strategic fit, and being able to defend the decision afterwards.

Writing the specification

A good PRD states the problem, the users, the success measure and what is explicitly out of scope. Most of its value is in agreeing what will not be built, because that is the part every stakeholder otherwise assumes is still coming.

Measuring afterwards

Shipping without a defined success metric means the outcome can only be argued about. Deciding what counts as a win has to happen before the work, not after.
