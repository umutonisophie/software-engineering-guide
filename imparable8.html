Product Design

Product design turns a requirement into something coherent, usable and buildable, and is accountable for how it feels as well as how it works.

Designing the thing, not the screen

A design covers states that are easy to omit: empty, loading, error, offline, and the case where a user has done something unexpected. Interfaces are mostly these edge states, not the happy path.

Working in systems

Components, tokens and consistent patterns mean a new feature inherits existing behaviour instead of reinventing it. Consistency is what makes an interface feel predictable.

Design and engineering are one conversation

A design that cannot be built within its constraints is a wish. The strongest work happens when the designer and the engineer argue it out before implementation starts.
