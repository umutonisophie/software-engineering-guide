UX Research

UX research studies how people actually use a product, so that design decisions rest on evidence rather than on the loudest opinion in the room.

What the method actually is

Interviews, observation, and usability testing, analysed for patterns. The output is not a set of preferences but a set of findings about behaviour, which is a much narrower and more useful claim.

Finding versus fixing

A study that produces a list of complaints has not done its job. The work is identifying the underlying problem - a user who cannot find the button may be blocked by terminology, not layout.

Honesty about evidence

A small usability test can tell you that people cannot complete a task. It cannot tell you the market will buy the product. Overstating a method's reach is the most common way research gets misused.
